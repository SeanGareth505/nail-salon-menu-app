Users

Any account in Firebase Authentication is an admin automatically — no Firestore profile required.

Admins sign in at /login with email and password. The salon tablet opens consent forms at /therapist — no therapist sign-in required.

How access works

  • Admins — sign in at /login with email & password (Firebase Auth).
  • Tablet — open /therapist on the salon iPad for consent forms. Therapists are selected during each form, not at login.

Add new admins in Firebase Console → Authentication → Add user, then they can sign in immediately.
