Workspace access

Manage the people who can access your salon workspace.

Use individual administrator accounts for salon management. The shared salon tablet has its own workspace for client consent forms.

How access works

  • Administrators sign in with their email and password to manage the menu, team, and salon settings.
  • Salon tablet opens the staff workspace for client forms. Select the treating therapist during the form.

Add new admins in Firebase Console → Authentication → Add user, then they can sign in immediately.
