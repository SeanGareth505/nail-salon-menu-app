# Default client consultation and consent, version 2

Prepared on 8 September 2026 from the three supplied photographs (reference pages 1–3 of a six-page booklet). Pages 4–6 were not supplied. The salon names, logos and third-party contact details from the reference have not been copied into the default.

The executable wording is in `src/assets/default-consent-template.json`. This is a draft for the salon's South African legal and treatment-practice review, not a certification that the terms are enforceable or appropriate for every service. Confirm the salon's legal identity, actual cancellation/refund practices, privacy notice and retention arrangements before operational use. A policy for minors/guardian consent and any treatment-specific contraindications or risk disclosures need separate review; this general form does not replace those procedures.

## Wording decisions

- The 10-minute arrival request and 24-hour cancellation notice are drawn from the supplied reference. The text does not invent a fee amount or state that every late cancellation incurs a charge.
- The original unrestricted release of liability has been replaced with a limited statement about disclosed inherent risks and outcomes caused solely by knowingly withheld information or failure to follow explained aftercare. It expressly preserves consumer rights and does not purport to exclude negligence, gross negligence, deliberate wrongdoing or liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.
- The disclosure statement does not erase historical records or automatically override earlier information; clients are asked to correct information that is no longer accurate.
- Treatment consent is specific to the visit. Staff must discuss the actual treatment, price, relevant risks, alternatives and aftercare before the client confirms those statements. A generic signed form is not a clinical suitability decision.
- Health questions offer Yes / No / Unsure, with conditional details and staff review prompts. Medication details have their own answer key. Optional routines and lifestyle questions may be skipped.
- Consent choices are not pre-checked. The form requires an affirmative acknowledgement rather than accepting an unchecked value as complete.
- The privacy wording is an explanation of the intended processing, not a substitute for the salon's complete privacy notice or a claim about retention/access controls that have not been verified.

## Sources checked

The Department of Trade, Industry and Competition's official Consumer Protection Act guide, April 2020, explains notice of risky terms and restrictions on unfair terms and gross-negligence exclusions (printed pages 25–27):

`https://www.thedtic.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Consumer_Protection_Act.pdf`

The Protection of Personal Information Act, hosted by the Department of Justice, including sections 11, 18, 26 and 27 on consent, notices and health information:

`https://www.justice.gov.za/legislation/acts/2013-004.pdf`

These sources informed the cautious wording above; they are not a substitute for advice on the salon's circumstances.

## Version and rollout

Version 1 remains unchanged for historical records. New sessions using the untouched built-in default receive version 2. Custom published forms, active custom drafts and disabled templates are preserved. In-progress drafts retain their form version. No production database seed, mass migration or hosting deployment is part of this change.
